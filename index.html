How to use NextStep

  1. Add each school, scholarship, exam, or program and its verified deadline.
  2. Update its status as you prepare and submit your application.
  3. Use the document checklist to track items you need to collect.
  4. Export a backup regularly; your information is stored only in this browser on this device.
Privacy: Entries stay in this browser and are not uploaded to an account. Clearing browser data or switching devices can remove them, so keep a backup.

Browser reminders work only when this app is open and notifications are allowed. For privacy, no cloud sync or email reminders are enabled.

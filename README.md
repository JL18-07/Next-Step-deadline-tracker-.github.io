# Next-Step

A deadline tracker designed for senior high school students to stay organized and manage important school deadlines in one place.

## Why Next-Step?

Balancing schoolwork, projects, quizzes, and exams can be overwhelming. Next-Step helps students keep track of deadlines, stay on top of assignments, and avoid last-minute stress.

## Features

- Add deadlines for assignments, projects, and exams
- Organize tasks by subject or category
- View upcoming and overdue deadlines
- Mark tasks as complete when finished
- Stay focused with a simple and easy-to-use layout

## How It Works

1. Add a task with its name and due date.
2. Organize it by subject or type.
3. Check your upcoming deadlines regularly.
4. Mark tasks complete as you finish them.

## Who Is It For?

This app is built for senior high school students who want a simple way to manage school responsibilities and keep track of deadlines.
